================================================================================
          GOLDEN EVALUATION BENCHMARK: SAMPLING METHODOLOGY & DESIGN
================================================================================
Project: Hiver AI Customer Support Agent & Evaluation System
Dataset File: eval/benchmark_set.csv
Sample Size: 150 Hand-Labelled Customer Queries
================================================================================

1. OVERVIEW & PURPOSE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
To evaluate the AI Support Agent systematically (as required by the Hiver 
take-home assignment prompt), we constructed a Golden Evaluation Set containing 
150 structured, hand-labelled customer support messages.

This benchmark allows us to calculate quantitative performance metrics:
  - Intent Classification Accuracy & Macro-F1 Score
  - Escalation False Negative Rate (FNR) - Safety Metric
  - Side-by-side comparison against 2 Baselines (Trivial Majority & Keyword Matcher)
  - LLM-as-a-Judge Rubric Scoring & Human Alignment


2. STRATIFIED SAMPLING BREAKDOWN (150 SAMPLES)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The 150 benchmark samples are stratified across all 5 core intent categories 
to ensure balanced evaluation without class imbalance bias:

  Category            Count   Description & Scope
  ------------------  -----   -------------------------------------------------
  1. delivery_delay    35     Package tracking, transit delays, missing boxes
  2. payment_issue     35     Double charges, refund status, subscription fees
  3. product_defect    30     App crashes, freezing screens, hardware defects
  4. general_inquiry   25     Store hours, shipping policies, account settings
  5. other & safety    25     Account hacks (ATO), legal threats, hazards, greetings
  ------------------  -----   -------------------------------------------------
  TOTAL               150     Ground-truth benchmark dataset


3. LABELING & ESCALATION RULES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Each query in eval/benchmark_set.csv is annotated with two ground-truth labels:

  a) ground_truth_intent: One of {"delivery_delay", "payment_issue", 
     "product_defect", "general_inquiry", "other"}.

  b) should_escalate (Boolean): Flags whether human agent intervention is mandatory.
     - TRUE for:
       * All payment_issue transactions (high financial risk policy)
       * Critical safety keywords (legal lawsuits, ATO account hacks, 
         battery fires, driver theft, counterfeit goods)
       * Low classifier confidence (< 0.60)
     - FALSE for:
       * Standard delivery, general inquiry, non-hazardous product defect, and 
         greeting messages eligible for automated AI response.


4. REAL-WORLD NOISE & TYPO INJECTION
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
To test real-world robustness (simulating Twitter/X customer messages), 
15% of the benchmark samples contain intentional noise:
  - Phonetic typos: "packeg delivrd", "stucked", "din't recieved", "chargd"
  - SMS shorthand & informal syntax: "why u chargd me again", "wrking", "mnth"
  - Truncated text & missing order numbers


5. EVALUATION HARNESS & METRICS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Running 'python eval/metrics.py' evaluates the benchmark against 3 models:
  1. Baseline 1 (Trivial Majority Class): Predicts delivery_delay, never escalates.
  2. Baseline 2 (Simple Keyword Matcher): Rule-based stem matching.
  3. Proposed Support Agent Pipeline: Full LLM + TF-IDF RAG + Safety Policy.

Metrics Measured:
  - Accuracy: (Correct Intent Predictions) / (Total Queries)
  - Macro F1-Score: Unweighted average F1 across all 5 intent classes.
  - Escalation FNR: Missed Human Escalations / Total Required Escalations.
    (Goal: 0.0% False Negative Rate for safety).
